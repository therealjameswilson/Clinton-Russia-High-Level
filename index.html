Collection Design

What This Page Houses

Leader Contacts

The Clinton Library foreign-leader chronology is used to seed a date-ordered list of Clinton-Yeltsin meetings and calls.

Release Packets

Clinton Library MDR releases are kept as first-class records so a compiler can move quickly from chronology to source packet.

Policy Context

Talbott FOIA records supply the diplomatic background around NATO/Russia, Kosovo, arms control, and Moscow consultations.

Catalog Leads

NARA Scout results track files that may contain additional memcons, telcons, withdrawals, or unprocessed research targets.
