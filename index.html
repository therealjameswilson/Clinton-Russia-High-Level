    Onsite Research Sprint

    The four 2013-0185-M finding-aid PDFs now drive a pull strategy for Clinton Library work: direct Yeltsin conversation files first, hard gaps second, and broad policy context only after source control is tight.

    Finding Aid Base

    • Four user-supplied 2013-0185-M finding-aid PDFs, 1,290 pages total.
    • 62,800 extracted text lines; 6,577 broad Russia/Yeltsin/contact hits.
    • 224 direct leader-contact keyword rows, 410 summit/trip rows, and 395 source-control rows.
    • Key marker families include kh2075, kh2076, kh2078, kh2080, kh2084, and kh2085.

    First Pulls

    • OA/ID 1466: Lake file, Memcons/Telcons [Russian President Boris Yeltsin].
    • OA/ID 70 and 72: Gati telephone/correspondence and Yeltsin/POTUS memcon summary folders.
    • OA/ID 2876 and 2877: Medish Russia-POTUS/Yeltsin meetings, correspondence, and telcons.
    • OA/ID 252 and 253: Burns chron folders for early Yeltsin calls and call memoranda.
    • OA/ID 3868 and 905: Gergen/Feeley Yeltsin telcon leads that may explain date discrepancies.

    Gap Chase

    • April 1, 1993 telcon: Lake 1466, Gati 70/72, Burns 253, Situation Room April 1993.
    • June 3, 1994 telcon: Lake 1466, Beyrle May-June 1994, POTUS Chron and Situation Room June 1994.
    • July 10, 1994 Naples memcon: Exec Sec 824, RUE 1025, POTUS Chron/Situation Room July 1994.
    • September 1998 Moscow memcon: RUE Admin 2609, Medish 2875/2877, Situation Room September 1998.
    • February 1999 Amman and June 2000 Moscow: Amman trip files, RUE 2614, Records Management trip books, Berger call/schedule files.

    Onsite Protocol

    • Confirm how Clinton Library staff translate 2013-0185-M OA/ID values into call slips.
    • For useful documents, capture the folder cover, withdrawal/marker sheet, actual document pages, and stamps/control data.
    • Extract only actual memcon or telcon pages for derivative PDFs, then append the marker/provenance sheet at the end.
    • Count conversation pages separately from marker pages, routing slips, talking points, briefing memos, and duplicate drafts.
